From encounter to documentation

A connected case workflow.

This is the structure we use to organize case material. The required sections and available evidence are confirmed for your version and course.

  1. 01

    History

    Questions asked and the patient's responses.

  2. 02

    Physical examination

    Examination choices and documented findings.

  3. 03

    Key findings

    The relevant positives and negatives.

  4. 04

    Problem statement

    A focused summary of the presentation.

  5. 05

    Differential diagnosis

    Compare and prioritize possible explanations.

  6. 06

    Tests and results

    Connect investigations to the clinical question.

  7. 07

    Diagnosis

    Explain how the evidence supports the assessment.

  8. 08

    Management

    Review treatment, education and follow-up.

  9. 09

    Documentation

    Organize the required case notes and course deliverables.
